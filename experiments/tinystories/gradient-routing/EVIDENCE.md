# Evidence ledger: sources frozen 2026-11-18
**Scope:** Published claims below are not measurements on the current workstation. Positive deltas mean worse held-out NLL than the ordinary head. These studies have different tasks, model sizes, rank interventions and seed policies; do not pool their effects.

A 2B backbone/rank study used factored forward heads; fastest high-rank arm reached narrow-arm loss earlier at this specific horizon. A different task or sufficient post-warmup budget might change these outcomes. |
