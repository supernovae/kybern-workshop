# Project Scope & Historical Archive

Kybern Workshop focuses on three active experiment tracks that you can inspect, replicate, and build on with [Tiny Sparse Lab](https://github.com/supernovae/tiny-sparse-lab):

1. **[Basic Synthetic CPU](../examples/synthetic-cpu/README.md)** — Fast offline workflow to verify your local install, data preparation, training loop, and reporting.
2. **[TinyStories](../examples/tinystories-microlab/README.md)** — Controlled architecture comparisons (`tinystories-microlab`) and our completed [8,192-update CPU learning baseline](../examples/tinystories-useful/README.md) with [27 recorded story outputs](../results/2026-10-19-tinystories-cpu/README.md).
3. **[DevMind](../experiments/devmind/README.md)** — **DevMind 340M Base** architecture specification and 24-phase corpus preparation recipes.

## Planned Research & Earlier Archive

Our upcoming research roadmap—covering conditional memory, fact swaps, context overrides, and dense-model controls—is published on **[kybern.dev/experiments](https://kybern.dev/experiments/#planned-experiments)**. Each new study is designed and run from scratch on current Tiny Sparse Lab interfaces.

Earlier exploratory studies and legacy experiment scaffolds have been archived out of the active branch to keep this repository focused and easy to navigate. If you need to reference files from those earlier runs, they remain preserved in Git history at snapshot [`fd259e2`](https://github.com/supernovae/kybern-workshop/tree/fd259e2cad4e9856d6d659e8df34728a98b32502/).
